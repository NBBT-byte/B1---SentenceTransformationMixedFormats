PET sentence transformation practice

B1 grammar workout

Twelve sets of five sentences, practised in five different formats

  • Section 1 · Set 1: choose the correct sentence
  • Section 2 · Set 3: fill in the missing word
  • Section 3 · Set 5: choose the missing word or phrase
  • Section 4 · Set 7: change the form of the word
  • Section 5 · Sets 9–10: rewrite the sentences using the word given
  • Extra section: 30 more questions from the other sets, unlocked when you have submitted sections 1 to 5

Do sections 1 to 5 in any order and move between them whenever you like. Press Submit in a section to see your score, the correct answers and the grammar behind each one. Use Try again to redo a section.
